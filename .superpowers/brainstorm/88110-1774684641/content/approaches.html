Three Design Approaches

Swiss-brutalist hybrid · monochrome · light only · collapsible sections

A

The Grid Sheet

Concept: The page is a strict 12-column grid with visible gridlines. Content snaps to columns. Large uppercase sans-serif headings sit in the left 4 columns, content fills the right 8. Section dividers are thick black horizontal rules. Collapsible sections use a simple [+]/[−] toggle.

Typography: One typeface family (e.g., Space Grotesk or Inter) at extreme size contrasts — 72px headings, 14px body.

Feel: Like a modernist newspaper or architectural blueprint. Very structured, almost mechanical.

Tradeoff: Most visually striking but most rigid — content must fit the grid or it feels broken.

B

The Specimen Page

Concept: Inspired by type specimen sheets. Your name is set massive (100px+) at the top. Below, content is arranged in clearly labeled blocks with heavy borders. Each section is a "specimen" — a self-contained card with a bold label. Collapsed sections show only the label bar.

Typography: Mix of a grotesque sans-serif for headings and a monospace for body/metadata. Size hierarchy is extreme.

Feel: Like a designer's portfolio meets a technical document. Bold but readable.

Tradeoff: Most balanced — strong identity without sacrificing readability. Slightly less austere than A.

C

The Index

Concept: The page reads like a structured index or table of contents. Minimal decoration — just text, lines, and whitespace. Navigation is a numbered list at the top. Sections are numbered (01, 02, 03...) with thin rule separators. Collapsible sections simply expand inline.

Typography: Single monospace typeface throughout. Hierarchy through size and weight only, no style tricks.

Feel: Like a terminal output or technical reference. Maximum austerity, almost anti-design.

Tradeoff: Most minimal and fastest to build, but risks feeling too plain for a personal homepage.

My recommendation: B (The Specimen Page) — it hits the sweet spot between Swiss structure and brutalist boldness while keeping your academic content readable.
